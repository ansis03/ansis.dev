Quantum computing

How training data affect a 3D-⁠CNN surface-⁠code decoder

For a 3D-CNN decoder at distance 5, sampling above the target error rate and label smoothing helped the most, while keeping the shots that matching fails on made it worse.

Under review at ICCDE 2027.

How training-⁠set sampling affects QCNN intrusion detection

Under a fixed training budget, spreading attack records evenly across types raises AUC but lowers recall at a 1% false-positive rate.

Under review at ICAIIC 2027.

Anomaly detection

Context-⁠aware anomaly detection in single-⁠person households

Daily routines from six ambient sensors, and anomaly detection within each person's own routine. Context changes which days get flagged, but a simpler global baseline scored higher.

Three papers and a registered program.
