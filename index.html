Interests

  • Quantum error correction
  • Decoding for qLDPC and surface codes
  • Quantum machine learning

I work on quantum computing, mainly decoders for quantum error correction and quantum machine learning. I'm an undergraduate in Computer Science and Engineering at Dongguk University in Seoul (expected to graduate in February 2028).

In error correction, I studied how training data affect a neural decoder for the surface code. I'm now working on how a real-time window decoder for qLDPC codes should protect the outcome of a logical measurement.

In quantum machine learning, I studied how the choice of training records affects quantum convolutional neural networks for intrusion detection.

The Research page has longer write-ups of finished projects, with figures, results, and what did not work. The qLDPC work will be added there once its paper is public.
